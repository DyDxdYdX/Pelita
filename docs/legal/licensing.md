# Application licensing

Pelita selected the [MIT license](../../LICENSE) on 10 November 2026 for its first-party source and associated documentation. The project chose MIT for permissive reuse and short notice requirements. [OSI license reference](https://opensource.org/license/mit)

Before public contributions or build distribution, confirm the copyright notice with the maintainers, publish inbound contribution terms, and review the resolved dependency and notice inventory against what actually ships. Include webview installers, codecs, converters and fonts if bundled.

Contributors ordinarily retain copyright in their contributions unless a separate arrangement changes ownership. Document inbound contribution terms and decide whether a Developer Certificate of Origin is appropriate before opening contributions. A code license covers the rights granted in code. Track content and assets separately and attach the required notices and permissions to each distributable pack. See [content permissions](content-permissions.md).
