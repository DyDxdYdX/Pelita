# Presenting a service

The **Preview** shows the selected playlist item privately. **Live output** shows what Pelita has sent to the audience. Selecting an item does not display it; **Take** does.

## Use an extended projector display

1. Connect the projector and set your computer to use an extended desktop.
2. Open **Projector** in the top bar, choose the audience display, then press **Confirm display**. The output opens in black. Check the physical screen before continuing.
3. Select the opening item in **Playlist**, review **Preview**, and press **Take**.
4. Use the **Slides** strip to jump within the selected live item. The gold **Live** label follows the slide as you use the strip, the **Live output** arrows, or the keyboard. Scroll the strip sideways to see more slides.
5. Press **Take** when it is time to switch from Scripture slides to the sermon presentation or another item.

To start a different item at a specific slide, select that item, choose its thumbnail in **Slides**, and then press **Take**. Preview remains private until Take. If you edit or regenerate a live deck, its updated thumbnails stay private until you take that version.

**Black** hides the current image temporarily. **Restore** reveals the current live slide. **Clear** removes the live item and stops its accompaniment. **Close output** in the **Projector** dialog closes the presentation window.

## Play the audio playlist

Select a track under **Audio playlist** and press **Play selected track**. Pelita advances through the saved order automatically. The transport at the bottom has play, pause, previous, next, restart, stop, seek, volume, and repeat controls. **Repeat all** wraps the list and **Repeat one** replays the current track at its end. **Repeat off** stops at the end of the list.

Playlist audio continues through slide changes, Black, Clear, and Takes of slides without accompaniment. Taking a slide item with an attached MP3 switches to that accompaniment. Taking a video pauses playlist playback. You can resume it after the video leaves the output. If a track fails, use **Next track** to continue or **Run preflight** to relink a missing file.

## Single or mirrored presentation

If no separate audience display is available, select a playlist item, open **Projector**, choose **Single / mirrored**, read the warning, and press **Open in black**. Pelita hides the operator window while presenting. Anyone watching the shared desktop can see the operator window before you enter this mode and after you exit.

Use **Enter** to Take the selected item, **Page Up** or **Page Down** to Take an adjacent playlist item, and **Esc** to leave this mode. Rehearse these controls before worship.

## Keyboard controls

With focus on the workspace, **Enter** Takes the selected item, **Left** and **Right** move between live slides, **B** toggles Black, **R** Restores, and **C** Clears. The arrow shortcuts also work while a thumbnail has focus. Select **Keyboard shortcuts** at the top of the app to see the current list.

## If the projector disconnects

Pelita suspends output when it loses the display. Reconnect the projector, open **Projector**, press **Refresh displays**, choose the intended display, and press **Confirm display** again. Verify the output is black, select the item you want, and press **Take**. Check the physical screen before resuming the service. See [Troubleshooting](troubleshooting.md) if the display does not reappear.
