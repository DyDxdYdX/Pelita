# Product vision

Small SDA congregations need a presentation workflow a volunteer can rehearse and run without internet access. Quelea works reliably in the originating church, but LPMI lyric videos cover only part of the collection. For other songs, operators advance PowerPoint lyrics while playing an MP3 separately in VLC. Some congregations project PowerPoint using duplicated or extended displays.

Pelita brings those workflows into one service queue. The first live-service target is a volunteer running a prepared Sabbath program with text, slides, video, and manually paced lyrics over local accompaniment. Keep live controls predictable and make recovery possible without leaving the operator screen.

## Audience and assumptions

- A volunteer prepares and operates the service on a church computer. One person can perform both roles.
- Churches may have modest hardware, intermittent connectivity, and either an extended desktop or a mirrored output.
- Media arrives as local files from sources the congregation manages. Format compatibility and permission to redistribute vary by asset.
- English ships first. Keep UI strings separate from song text and preserve Unicode content for future localization.
- The product is SDA-focused, independently maintained, and modular. Quelea feature parity and expansion to other denominations are outside the initial goal.

At pilot rehearsals, observe whether a volunteer can prepare, reopen, and run a service without another playback app, find a song by number, and recover from a display interruption using the on-screen controls. See [workflows](user-workflows.md) and [MVP scope](mvp-scope.md).
